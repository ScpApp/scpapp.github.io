Reporting an issue

Include the details that help us reproduce the issue:

  • App version: v1.3.1
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • The steps that caused the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • For MIDI issues, your keyboard model and connection type.

Files and MIDI playback

Open compatible Yamaha style files (.sty, .prs, .sst) and standard MIDI files (.mid, .midi). Editing works without a keyboard. To hear keyboard voices and effects, connect a compatible MIDI instrument and select it as MIDI Out. The app does not include the keyboard’s sound library.

Style Creator PRO is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yamaha. Manufacturer and model names identify compatibility.

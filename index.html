Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Style Creator PRO is an arranger style and MIDI editor that works without an account. This policy explains how the app handles your files, preferences, and optional file transfers.

Data handled by the app

The app processes your files, MIDI input, and preferences on your device. It does not send your projects, MIDI performance, usage activity, or device identifiers to the developer. It contains no advertising or analytics services and does not track you.

Files, preferences, and MIDI

Imported files, saved styles, voice favorites, MIDI mappings, and app preferences are stored locally. MIDI input is used to record notes and control playback. The app does not record the microphone or access your photos, contacts, or location. Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Optional Wi-Fi file transfer

Opening Wi-Fi File Transfer starts a temporary server on your local network. A browser with the access code can manage the files in Imports and Saved Styles, including uploading, downloading, renaming, moving, and deleting them. Local network addresses and temporary session credentials are used to make this connection. The browser uses a session cookie for access and local storage for its theme; these are not used for tracking. Transfers use unencrypted HTTP. Use a private network you trust. Closing the transfer window or putting the app in the background stops the server and invalidates access. You can also revoke Local Network access in system Settings; local editing and the system file picker remain available.

Retention and deletion

Files and preferences remain on your device until you remove them. To delete a library file, open the file picker, enter Imports or Saved Styles, and use Delete. On iPad, Delete App removes the app’s local files and preferences; Offload App keeps them. Exported copies and backups must be removed separately from their storage locations. The developer cannot directly access or delete files on your device.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you include. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by your mail provider and the support mailbox provider. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below. Do not attach private files unless you want to share them.

Online policy and support pages

If you open the online privacy or support pages, your browser connects to the hosting service. Hosting providers may process standard request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure those pages. The pages provided by the developer do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or account registration.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support email, contact us to have it deleted.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support.
